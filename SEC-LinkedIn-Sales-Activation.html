05 · Why the DMs Actually Get Replies

Most outreach fails before
a single word is read.

Princeton researchers proved in 2006 that trust decisions happen in under one-tenth of a second. Your prospect's inbox runs three gates before their conscious brain processes anything. Fail any one of them and the message is dead. Before they read your credentials, before they saw your proof, before they reached your offer. None of it mattered. They had already moved on.

Gate 1
Is this real?
Not a bot. Not a VA with a merge field. Not a copy-paste message sent to 400 people with a name swapped in. Tribal recognition in the opening tells them: a real person who has been inside their world wrote this.
Gate 2
Is this for me?
Not something that could have been sent to 500 people who share a job title. The personalised value piece is Gate 2 in physical form: it proves this message was written for one person only. No template generates what we build.
Gate 3
Is this safe?
Is this person about to extract something from me, or show up with something useful? The permission close creates full freedom to say no. That is exactly what creates yes. No pressure. No countdown. No artificial urgency.

Every DM in the Sprint is engineered to pass all three gates before the first line is finished. The personalised value piece, the tribal recognition in the opening, the permission close: each one solves a specific gate. Together, they produce the only result that matters: a reply from someone worth talking to.

"We do not send the Unclogged Toilet. The specific problem they have but have not named yet. Identified and solved before they even asked. That is what gets the reply."

Questions We Get Asked

Everything you need to know
before you book.

What is LinkedIn Sales Activation?

LinkedIn Sales Activation is a done-for-you outreach system that turns your existing LinkedIn connections into qualified sales conversations. Sales Edge Creators exports your LinkedIn connections, runs them through a proprietary AI pipeline to extract your actual ICP from your real network, builds a custom lead magnet for each high-probability prospect anchored to their buying signals and your value proposition, writes a matching personalised DM, and delivers 10 to 20 of these packages every weekday. The result is a consistent flow of warm, relevant sales conversations without posting content, cold outreach, or paid ads.

How does Sales Edge Creators find buyers inside my LinkedIn network?

You export your LinkedIn connections as a CSV file and send it to us. Our proprietary multi-layer AI pipeline does not apply a preset filter. It extracts your actual ICP from your connection pool by scoring every person on role fit, offer fit, timing signals, and buying intent specific to your business. The output is a ranked prospect list drawn exclusively from people already connected to you. These are warm relationships, not cold strangers, which is why the reply rates are significantly higher than cold outreach.

What is a custom lead magnet in the context of LinkedIn outreach?

A custom lead magnet is a small, high-relevance piece of value built specifically for one prospect. It is anchored to that person's role, their likely pain points based on buying and intent signals, and your offer's specific value proposition. It solves an itch they are already experiencing rather than promoting what you do generically. The accompanying DM references this lead magnet directly. When someone receives a message that speaks precisely to their situation and offers something genuinely useful, they reply. That is the mechanism behind the 15 to 35 percent reply rates on this system.

Do I need Sales Navigator or a large LinkedIn following?

No. This system runs entirely on your first-degree LinkedIn connections. You do not need Sales Navigator, a large audience, or any existing content presence. The buyers are already connected to you. The system identifies who they are, builds the right approach for each one, and starts the conversations. If you have fewer than 200 first-degree connections, we can discuss whether a connection-building phase makes sense before running the sprint.

What is the difference between Optimisation + Sprint and Sprint Only?

Optimisation + Sprint ($2,000/month) includes a full LinkedIn profile rebuild using Lincoln's Radical Subtraction session to clarify your offer, ICP, and positioning before the sprint begins. This is for coaches, consultants, and B2B founders whose profile is not yet converting visitors into enquiries. Sprint Only ($1,500/month) is for people whose profile is already working and who want to activate the daily outreach intelligence system immediately. Both tiers include the CSV-based ICP extraction, custom lead magnets, custom DMs, and 10 to 20 packages built per weekday.

What does Full Inbox Management include?

Full Inbox Management is a $500/month add-on available on either tier. With it, our team sends all DMs on your behalf, manages follow-ups, handles replies, and continuously adds qualified new connections to your network so your pool never runs dry. You receive warm qualified conversations in your inbox without touching LinkedIn at all. Many clients choose this once the outreach rhythm is established and they want to remove themselves from the sending process entirely.

How quickly can I expect sales conversations to start?

Sprint Only clients typically see the first replies within the first week of outreach beginning. Optimisation + Sprint clients start outreach as soon as the profile build is complete, which typically runs in parallel with your intake form. Projected ranges from the full system are 30 to 140 replies per month and 6 to 18 qualified conversations opened monthly, based on a warm network and 10 to 20 daily outreach packages. These are projections. Results depend on your offer, your network quality, and your close rate on conversations opened.

Is this better than posting content on LinkedIn?

Content builds awareness over time. Outreach starts conversations immediately. Most coaches and consultants who are not yet generating consistent clients from LinkedIn are better served by starting direct, personalised conversations with the warm buyers already in their network before investing weeks into a content calendar. Sales happens in conversations, not in posts. Once you have a predictable flow of conversations opening, content compounds on top of that foundation. This system is designed to get you to qualified conversations first.

Who is this not right for?

This system is not right for you if you do not yet have a defined offer or a clear sense of who you help, if you have fewer than 200 first-degree LinkedIn connections and are unwilling to do a connection-building phase first, or if your business model relies on high-volume low-ticket transactions that do not suit a personalised conversation-based approach. We run a fit diagnostic on the discovery call. If this is not the right fit for your situation, we will tell you directly.
